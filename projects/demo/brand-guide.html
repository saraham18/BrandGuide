Stripe

Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue.

Mission

To increase the GDP of the internet by building the most powerful and flexible financial infrastructure for businesses of every size, from first transaction to billionth.

Stripe is the backbone of global commerce - a developer-first, enterprise-grade payments and financial platform that lets businesses accept payments, build billing models, issue cards, move money globally, and embed financial services into any product. It combines technical depth with elegant simplicity, trusted by startups and 50% of Fortune 100 companies alike.

Audience

Target Audience

Founders, CTOs, product managers, and finance leaders at high-growth startups, scaling SaaS companies, marketplaces, and enterprise organizations who need reliable, programmable financial infrastructure without operational friction.

A 30-40 year old head of payments or revenue operations at a fast-scaling e-commerce or platform business, managing multi-currency transactions and international expansion. They care about conversion rates, uptime, and borderless money movement.

The Developer Builder

A 22-32 year old full-stack engineer who is the internal champion for Stripe adoption, energized by clean APIs and excellent documentation. They evaluate tools by how quickly they can ship a proof of concept and how far the platform can scale.

Style

Voice & Tone

Precise, confident, and quietly ambitious.

Write with technical authority but never jargon for jargon's sake. Lead with scale and capability, then ground it in concrete numbers and real outcomes. Sentences should be tight and declarative - no fluff, no hype. Use active voice. Speak to builders and decision-makers as peers. Avoid superlatives unless backed by data. The tone is calm confidence: Stripe doesn't need to shout because the numbers speak. When writing CTAs, be direct and action-oriented ('Start building', 'See the docs', 'Talk to sales').

Casting Notes

Cast real-looking founders, engineers, and operators aged 25-45 - diverse in background, sharp in energy, working in real environments: modern open offices, home setups with multiple monitors, co-working spaces, or retail floors. Avoid overly polished or stock-photo aesthetics. Subjects should look like they're actually building something, not posing. Wardrobe: smart-casual, functional. Energy: focused, purposeful, quietly excited - not loud or salesy. For enterprise spots, include team settings and boardroom-adjacent contexts that signal scale without feeling stiff.

Rules

DO: Use real metrics and proof points in copy whenever possible. DO: Reflect the full spectrum of business sizes - from solo founders to Fortune 100. DO: Pair technical capability with business outcome language. DO: Use the deep indigo (#533afd) as the dominant brand signal in visuals. DO: Show the product (dashboard, API, checkout) in context - not as abstract UI. DO: Maintain visual cleanliness - generous whitespace, structured layouts. DON'T: Use generic 'payments made easy' language - Stripe is infrastructure, not a widget. DON'T: Overpromise or use unsubstantiated superlatives. DON'T: Show chaotic, cluttered, or low-fidelity creative - quality signals trust. DON'T: Use humor that undermines credibility - wit is fine, slapstick is not. DON'T: Ignore developer and technical audiences in favor of pure business messaging. DON'T: Use competitor names or comparative claims. DON'T: Depict payment failure, fraud, or downtime scenarios - reinforce reliability always.
