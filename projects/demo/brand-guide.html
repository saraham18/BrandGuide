SKIMS

Solutions For Every Body

Mission

To create the next generation of underwear, clothing, and shapewear that solves real fit and comfort problems for every body type.

SKIMS is a solutions-oriented intimates and loungewear brand founded by Kim Kardashian. It leads with inclusive sizing, body-conscious construction, and a neutral-to-cool aesthetic that feels modern and aspirational without being exclusionary. The brand spans shapewear, underwear, bras, swim, and collaborative collections like NikeSKIMS.

Audience

Target Audience

Women ages 18-45 across all body types and sizes who want underwear, shapewear, and loungewear that actually fits, smooths, and supports without sacrificing style or comfort.

Creative Personas

The Confident Curator

A woman in her late 20s to mid-30s who builds outfits intentionally and treats her foundational pieces as seriously as her outerwear. She shops for quality and fit first, and she is not afraid to invest in basics that make everything else look better.

The Comfort Seeker

A busy woman in her 30s or 40s who has given up on uncomfortable shapewear and wants solutions that move with her body through long days. She values softness, stretch, and a smooth silhouette without the squeeze.

The Body-Positive Explorer

A woman in her early 20s who is actively building a wardrobe that celebrates her shape rather than hiding it. She follows inclusive brands closely and shares her finds with an engaged online community.

The Elevated Minimalist

A style-conscious woman who gravitates toward neutral tones and clean lines and sees SKIMS as the brand that finally made basics feel luxurious. She layers SKIMS pieces into everyday outfits and expects them to perform as well as they photograph.

Style

Voice & Tone

Confident, inclusive, and quietly aspirational

Write in clean, direct sentences that feel empowering without being loud. Lead with the solution the product provides, not just the feature. Avoid clinical or overly technical language. Use body-positive framing that is matter-of-fact rather than preachy. Headlines should be short and declarative. Copy should feel like it was written by someone who genuinely wears and believes in the product.

Casting Notes

Cast women ages 18-50 across a full spectrum of body types, skin tones, and sizes - no single body type should dominate. Energy should be calm, self-assured, and natural rather than performative or high-energy. Settings should feel real and lived-in: a bright bedroom, a clean bathroom mirror, a modern kitchen, a studio with soft natural light. Avoid overly styled or editorial looks that feel unattainable. UGC talent should feel like a real customer who discovered something that works, not a model selling a product.

Rules

DO: Show the product on diverse real bodies in motion and at rest. DO: Highlight fit, smoothness, and comfort as the core visual story. DO: Use the brand's cool neutral palette as the backdrop for all creative. DO: Let the product speak through honest before-and-after or wear-it-your-way framing. DO: Reference specific product features like compression level, seamless construction, or silhouette when relevant. DON'T: Use overly retouched or homogenous body types that contradict the inclusive brand promise. DON'T: Use loud or aggressive sales language - SKIMS does not shout. DON'T: Introduce colors or fonts outside the brand palette in creative assets. DON'T: Portray shapewear as something to hide or be ashamed of - frame it as a tool of confidence. DON'T: Overcrowd copy; white space and restraint are core to the brand aesthetic.
