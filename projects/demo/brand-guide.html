Death Wish Coffee

The Society of Strong Coffee

Mission

To deliver the boldest, smoothest, highest-caffeine coffee on earth using USDA Organic and Fair Trade Certified beans, sourced sustainably and roasted to uncompromising standards for people who refuse to settle for ordinary.

Death Wish Coffee is a dark, mythology-charged coffee brand built for high-performers, rebels, and caffeine maximalists. Founded in 2012, it blends premium arabica and robusta beans from India, Peru, and Central and South America into a product that is simultaneously intense and smooth. The brand extends into lifestyle merchandise, limited-edition collectibles, and a subscriber community that rewards loyalty with exclusive access. Every touchpoint - from product names like Power Surge and Gates of Valhalla to copy like 'harness your inner storm' - signals that this is coffee as identity, not just caffeine.

Audience

Target Audience

Adults 22-45 who identify as hard workers, night owls, gym-goers, gamers, outdoor adventurers, or counterculture enthusiasts. They distrust corporate polish, respect craft and intensity, and want a brand that matches their energy. They respond to mythology, dark aesthetics, and community belonging.

A 30-45 year old who found Death Wish years ago and never looked back. They evangelize the brand to friends, leave detailed reviews, and feel genuine pride in being part of a coffee society that others have not discovered yet.

The High-Caffeine Convert

A 22-32 year old who was drinking mediocre coffee until one bag of Death Wish rewired their expectations entirely. They are early in their brand relationship but already evangelical, driven by the shock of discovering something genuinely different.

Style

Voice & Tone

Dark, bold, and mythic with a streak of earned confidence

Write like someone who has been through something and come out stronger. Use short, punchy declarative sentences alongside occasional epic, world-building phrases. Reference storms, endurance, power, and folklore without being campy. Never use corporate softness or hedging language. Avoid words like 'journey,' 'solution,' or 'innovative.' Superlatives are earned here - use them when the product backs them up. Reviews and community voices should feel raw and real, not polished. Discount and offer copy should feel like a reward, not a sale.

Casting Notes

Cast real-looking humans aged 24-42 with visible personality and physical presence - calloused hands, tattoos, worn flannel, gym chalk, or dark streetwear all work. Avoid model-perfect faces and sterile environments. Settings should feel lived-in: dim kitchens at 5am, cluttered workshops, foggy morning trails, moody apartments with bookshelves. Energy should be calm intensity rather than hyperactive. Women and men equally, with strong representation of military, trades, and athletic lifestyles. Avoid anyone who looks like they belong in a generic lifestyle stock photo.

Rules

DO use dark backgrounds, deep reds, and warm amber tones to anchor visuals. DO lean into mythology, folklore, and elemental metaphors. DO let customer voices be raw and unpolished in testimonial creative. DO create urgency around limited-edition drops with specific product names. DO reward the audience - they hate being talked down to. DO NOT use bright, airy, or pastel aesthetics that conflict with the brand's dark identity. DO NOT use generic coffee tropes like steam rising over a sunrise or a smiling barista. DO NOT soften the intensity of the product - the strength is the point. DO NOT use emoji or exclamation-point-heavy copy that reads as hype without substance. DO NOT misrepresent certifications - always pair organic and fair trade claims with factual grounding. DO NOT ignore the merchandise line - it is a core part of the brand identity, not an afterthought.
